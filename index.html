A gap in the wall

Killian Riley and Vanessa Callow spent their teenage years in neighbouring flats in Brentwell, talking through a hole the size of a fifty pence piece. It was enough to fall in love, and enough to make promises neither of them were old enough to keep.

Then one Boxing Day changed everything. Ten years later Killian owns St-Ink, the most sought after tattoo studio in London, and Vanessa is V.E. Callow, a bestselling children's author. When they end up in the same room for the first time in a decade, everything they buried comes back at once.

About L.J.

L.J. Newton writes contemporary romance and paranormal memoir from the North East of England: love, loss, fresh starts and the bravery it takes to begin again, with a dark sense of humour.

She keeps her stories tied to real things. Violet's chickens are real, and so is her bread. “If it feels real to me it will feel real to the reader. That is the theory anyway.”
